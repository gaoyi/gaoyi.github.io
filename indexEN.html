Representative work

Two highlights spanning the two pillars of my work.

AI-Assisted Automated Breast Ultrasound Screening

AI-assisted ABUS systems for large-scale screening, deployed in over 70 medical institutions and serving more than 90,000 people. The "3D Breast Ultrasound Image Management Software" obtained a Class II Medical Device Registration Certificate — the first of its kind in China.

Grow From Seed: Interactive 3D Segmentation

An end-user oriented interactive 3D image segmentation tool, a core interactive segmentation component of 3D Slicer since 2014. Published in Scientific Reports (2024; 14:29878). Supports MRI, CT, and ultrasound.
