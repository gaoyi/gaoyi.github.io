Pillar One · Research

Research

My work is organized in two parallel pillars: research — presented below both by clinical application and by method — and open science and software. The engine underneath is medical artificial intelligence, from mathematical modeling theory through open-source algorithms to clinical deployment and industrial translation.

01 · By Application

By Application

What clinical problems we solve, and the clinical outcome.

01

Breast Cancer

  • AI-assisted ABUS screening at scale: automated breast ultrasound systems deployed in 70+ medical institutions, screening more than 90,000 people; detection rate raised from 0.5‰ to 3.13‰.
  • Remote-reading screening model: prospective study across 46 community health centers in China (AJR 2024); lossy-compression scheme for remote screening of 3D volumetric ultrasound.
  • Medical device certification: "3D Breast Ultrasound Image Management Software" obtained a Class II Medical Device Registration Certificate — the first of its kind in China.
  • Molecular prediction and treatment response: explainable multi-task deep learning for molecular expression prediction from 3D whole-breast ultrasound; neoadjuvant treatment response assessment.
  • MRI quantification: reproducible automated breast density measurement (fat-water decomposition MRI); sentinel lymph node metastasis prediction from DCE-MRI.
02

Brain Science

  • Hippocampal morphology: fine-scale shape analysis across 2,911 subjects (multi-dataset) and 552 healthy subjects aged 20–80; "bumps under the hippocampus" anatomy characterized.
  • Cerebellar reconstruction: 3D digital reconstruction of the cerebellar cortex — lobule thickness, surface area, and layer architecture (Cerebellum 2022; reviewer: "an exceptional technical achievement").
  • Fluid transport modeling: cerebrospinal and interstitial fluid transport via the glymphatic pathway modeled by optimal mass transport (NeuroImage cover highlight, 2017).
  • Clinical neuro AI: MRI-based deep learning diagnosis of neonatal acute bilirubin encephalopathy; neurocognition after hydrocephalus treatment.
  • Ongoing: respiration and sleep analysis, EEG analysis, and transcranial Doppler-based cerebral blood flow assessment.
03

Spine and Orthopedics

  • Surgical outcome simulation: combining 3D body-surface scanning with CT volumetric data for ankylosing spondylitis surgery outcome simulation (IEEE ISBI 2024).
  • Thoracic spine enhancement: imaging enhancement techniques for thoracic vertebrae.
  • Image stitching: wide-field surface and volumetric image stitching for spinal applications.
  • Finite element analysis of bone structure for surgical planning.

Ongoing work in close collaboration with clinical spine surgery teams.
04

Computational Pathology

  • Open platform: an open-source platform for computational histopathology (IEEE Access 2021).
  • Molecular-histological correlation: histology image analysis of 13 healthy tissues (Scientific Reports 2025).
  • Segmentation and classification: nucleus segmentation, gland segmentation, prostate cancer classification; large-scale image synthesis for evaluation.
  • Review: computational pathology — recent developments in digital and intelligent pathology (Intelligent Oncology 2025).
05

Other Clinical Areas

  • Multi-disease AI: whole-body bone scintigraphy lesion detection; leptomeningeal metastasis classification on CSF cytomorphology; pediatric adenoid and nasopharyngeal airway segmentation.
  • Critical care and beyond: risk stratification models for acute kidney injury; COVID-19 lung targets.
  • Cardiac imaging (earlier work): left atrium, myocardium, and scar-tissue segmentation from MR/CT.

02 · By Method

By Method

How we solve them — the algorithmic and technical contributions behind the applications.

01

Interactive 3D Image Segmentation

  • Grow From Seed: local-statistics driven interactive segmentation, a core module of 3D Slicer since 2014 (Scientific Reports 2024).
  • Robust Statistics Segmenter: robust-statistics active contours for multi-object 3D segmentation (Medical Image Analysis 2012).
  • Learning-based segmentation: attention-enhanced scribble supervision; deep equilibrium models; multi-task networks with boundary regression; deep active learning for annotation efficiency.

Software released as open source — see Open Science and Software.
02

Deformable Image Registration

  • Diffusion-model and Mamba-based networks for deformation vector field generation and registration performance enhancement (IJIST 2024).
  • Point-set registration via filtering in the diffeomorphism group (IEEE TIP 2012).
  • Atlas and shape-prior based registration for multi-atlas segmentation (Kalman-filtering perspective, SIAM J. Imaging Sciences 2015).
03

Medical Image Synthesis

  • Distribution-preserving synthesis: diffusion probabilistic models for synthesizing medical images with annotations, improving segmentation performance (IJIST 2025; IEEE BIBM 2023).
  • Synthesis for validation: large-scale nucleus-segmentation evaluation through image synthesis (SPIE 2017).
04

Statistical Shape Analysis

  • 3D Poisson-equation morphometry with a quantitative validation framework (Medical Image Analysis 2016).
  • Multi-scale wavelet representation for 3D shapes of arbitrary topology (Medical Image Analysis 2012).
  • Spherical wavelets and conformal flattening — ITK filters for shape analysis on manifolds.
  • Validation methodology: shape analysis as "a field in need of careful validation" (Human Brain Mapping 2014).

Software: SPoM · multi-scale shape (see Open Science).
05

Radiomics and Predictive Modeling

  • Ultrasound radiomics for predicting germline BRCA mutations in breast cancer (Cancer Imaging 2024).
  • Treatment response prediction: anti-PD1 response in esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (Thoracic Cancer 2023); chemoimmunotherapy outcomes in extensive-stage small cell lung cancer (JITC 2023).
  • Quantitative imaging harmonization: model-based harmonization of PET standard uptake values for paired studies (Phenomics, in press).
